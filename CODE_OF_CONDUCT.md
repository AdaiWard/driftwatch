# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in DriftWatch a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, and focused on the work. Assume good intent, accept feedback gracefully, and give others room to learn. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, threats, and publishing private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner. The maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public spaces. It is adapted from the principles of the Contributor Covenant.
